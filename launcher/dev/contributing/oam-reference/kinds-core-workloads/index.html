Kinds: core workloads

Implementation, history and tests of the entries on the reference page Kinds: core workloads. Text in square brackets is added here to say what a moved sentence refers to.

webservice, worker

  • History:
    • go-kure/launcher#341 (the DeploymentSpec-level surface that webservice and worker share with deployment).
    • go-kure/launcher#1473 (webservice and worker publish tolerations and topologySpreadConstraints).
    • go-kure/launcher#280 (worker and webservice are component lowering rules, not handlers).
    • go-kure/launcher#702 (both rules emit the component’s ServiceAccount as a serviceaccount member and turn each claim a pvc volume describes into a synthesized pvc trait).
    • go-kure/launcher#546 (a webservice component name must be a valid Service name).
    • go-kure/launcher#959 (a leading digit in a webservice component name under RelaxedServiceNameValidation).
    • go-kure/launcher#1230 (the same rule below Kubernetes 1.36 and the spec.featureGates setting).
    • go-kure/launcher#787 (deploymentObjectName, serviceObjectName and serviceAccountObjectName).
    • go-kure/launcher#545 (an ingress portName on a webservice must be http).

deployment

  • History:
    • go-kure/launcher#412 (the raw corev1 scheduling surface: affinity, tolerations and topologySpreadConstraints).

service

  • History:
    • go-kure/launcher#411 (the kind-named Service, an independent component).
    • go-kure/launcher#383 (targetPort is published as the Types: [integer, string] union).
    • go-kure/launcher#790 (type: ExternalName, the further ServiceSpec fields, and the refusal of externalIPs, clusterIPs and a literal clusterIP address).
    • go-kure/launcher#1230 (PreferSameZone and PreferSameNode below Kubernetes 1.34, and a leading digit in the Service name below Kubernetes 1.36).
    • go-kure/launcher#690 (a headless Service, clusterIP: None).
    • go-kure/launcher#959 (a leading digit in the Service name under RelaxedServiceNameValidation).
    • go-kure/launcher#794 (service has no ApplyPolicy, deliberately).
    • go-kure/launcher#922 (the object kind policy, oam.ObjectKindPolicy).

serviceaccount, persistentvolumeclaim, configmap

  • History:
    • go-kure/launcher#702 (serviceaccount, persistentvolumeclaim and configmap as kind-named projections of one object each).
    • go-kure/launcher#741 (the pvc and configmap traits as twins of the kinds).
    • go-kure/launcher#742 (an unauthored storageClassName comes from the ClusterProfile pvc capability).
    • go-kure/launcher#790 (the four claim-spec fields selector, dataSourceRef, volumeName and volumeAttributesClassName).
    • go-kure/launcher#794 (serviceaccount and configmap have no ApplyPolicy, deliberately).
    • go-kure/launcher#922 (the object kind policy, oam.ObjectKindPolicy).

secret

  • History:
    • go-kure/launcher#790 (secret as the kind-named projection of a v1 Secret).

namespace, limitrange, resourcequota

  • History:
    • go-kure/launcher#790 (namespace, limitrange and resourcequota as kind-named projections of one core object each).
    • go-kure/launcher#788 (the transform sets the component label on every object a component owns).
  • Tests:
    • TestCoreKindSchemas_CoverSpec holds each schema to the linked type by reflection. [“each schema” is the schema of namespace, limitrange and resourcequota, and the test is in core_kinds_test.go]

persistentvolume

  • History:
    • go-kure/launcher#790 (persistentvolume as the kind-named projection of a v1 PersistentVolume).
    • go-kure/launcher#794 (csi and flexVolume sources are not covered by the hostPath gate, a decided limit).
